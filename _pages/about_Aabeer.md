Aabeer is a Ph.D. student in the Aggie CIS Lab with a research focus on coastal dynamics and sustainability. He completed his B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Water Resources Engineering at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). Before starting his Ph.D., he worked as a lecturer in the Department of Water Resources Engineering at BUET. For his M.Sc thesis, he explored the settling behavior of non-spherical microplastics in turbulent open-channel flow. He also worked on several other projects related to flood risk mapping and forecasting, coastal erosion, sediment transport, and shoreline dynamics.

When he is not working, Aabeer enjoys traveling and playing basketball.
