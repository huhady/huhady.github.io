Md. Tawhidur Rahaman is a Ph.D. student in the Aggie CIS Lab with a research focus on surrogate modeling for coastal processes and wave dynamics. He earned a B.Sc. in Civil Engineering and an M.Sc. in Water Resources Engineering from Bangladesh, where his academic training focused on hydrodynamics, coastal processes, and environmental systems. Before joining TAMU, he had over a decade of experience in environmental and water resources engineering, having contributed to international research and development projects supported by the World Bank, the Asian Development Bank, the Danish International Development Agency (Danida), the Swedish International Development Cooperation Agency (Sida), and the Gates Foundation. He is also a Beahrs Environmental Leadership Program Fellow at the University of California, Berkeley, and an UNLEASH Global Talent, where he contributes to interdisciplinary leadership and innovation for sustainable development.
